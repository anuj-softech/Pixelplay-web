infoAbout PixelPlay

PixelPlayer is a free, open-source Android video player built on the robust Media3 ExoPlayer architecture. Engineered with a built-in offline AI subtitle generator powered by the whisper.cpp engine, it offers completely private, real-time audio transcription. PixelPlay seamlessly delivers broad multi-format compatibility and elite performance metrics inside an ultra-lightweight environment.
